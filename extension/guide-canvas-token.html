Method 1: Quick Sign-In (Recommended)

This is the easiest way to connect your Canvas account. When you first install the extension, you'll be guided through this process automatically.

1

Open the Welcome Screen

After installing the Canvas AI Assistant extension, a welcome screen will automatically appear. If it doesn't, click the extension icon in your browser toolbar.

🖼️ Welcome Screen Preview
2

Click "Get Started"

On the welcome screen, you'll see a prominent "Get Started" button. Click it to begin the authentication process.

3

Sign In with Canvas

A Canvas login page will open. Enter your school credentials to authenticate. This securely connects your Canvas account to the extension.

ℹ️
Privacy Note: Your login credentials are never stored by the extension. We only receive an access token from Canvas.
4

Authorize the Extension

After logging in, Canvas will ask you to authorize the Canvas AI Assistant. Click "Authorize" to grant access to your course data.

5

You're All Set!

Once authorized, you'll be redirected back and the extension will begin syncing your Canvas data. You should see a success message confirming the connection.

✅ Connection successful! Your Canvas data is now syncing.
or
2

Method 2: Manual Token Entry

If the quick sign-in method doesn't work, you can manually enter your Canvas API token. This method gives you more control over token permissions and expiration.

1

Access Manual Login Option

On the welcome screen, scroll down past the "Get Started" button. You'll see an option that says "Enter token manually" or "Having trouble? Click here".

2

Open Authentication Settings

Click the manual option and you'll be redirected to the Authentication Setup page in the extension settings.

3

Enter Your Canvas Token

In the "Canvas API Token" section, paste your token into the input field. Don't have a token yet? See the section below on how to generate one.

•••••••••••••••••••• 👁️
4

Test & Save

Click "Test Connection" to verify your token works, then click "Save Settings". Reload any Canvas tab to start syncing.

3

How to Find Your Token Manually

If you need to generate a Canvas API token manually, follow these steps within Canvas:

1

Go to Canvas

Open Canvas in your browser and make sure you're logged in to your account.

2

Open Account Menu

Click on "Account" in the left sidebar navigation. It's located near the top, just below your school's logo (the Seahorse logo for Christ Church School).

🏫 Dashboard
👤 Account
📚 Courses
📅 Calendar
3

Navigate to Settings

From the Account menu, click on "Settings".

4

Find Access Tokens Section

Scroll down the Settings page until you find the "Approved Integrations" or "Access Tokens" section.

5

Create New Access Token

Click the "+ New Access Token" button.

+ New Access Token
6

Configure Your Token

A dialog will appear asking you to configure the token:

  • Purpose: Enter "Canvas AI Assistant" or any name you'll remember
  • Expires: Set an expiration date (optional but recommended for security)
⚠️
Important: We recommend setting an expiration date (e.g., end of semester) for security. You can always generate a new token later.
7

Generate and Copy Token

Click "Generate Token". Your new token will be displayed. Copy it immediately - you won't be able to see it again!

🚨
Critical: Copy your token NOW! Canvas only shows it once. If you lose it, you'll need to generate a new one.
8

Paste in Extension Settings

Go back to the Canvas AI Assistant settings and paste your token in the Canvas API Token field. Click "Save Settings" to complete setup.

4

Verifying Your Connection

After setting up your token, here's how to confirm everything is working:

✓

Status Badge Shows "Connected"

In the settings page, the Canvas API Token card should show a green "✅ Connected" badge.

✓

Extension Popup Shows Data

Click the extension icon - you should see your courses count and upcoming assignments.

✓

Chatbot Responds with Your Data

Open the AI chatbot on any Canvas page and ask "What assignments are due this week?" - it should show your actual assignments.

🎉
Success! If all checks pass, you're all set to use Canvas AI Assistant. Try asking the chatbot about your courses, grades, or upcoming deadlines!
