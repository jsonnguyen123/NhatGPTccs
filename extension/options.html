About Canvas AI Assistant

Canvas AI Assistant

Version 1.0.0

Canvas AI Assistant is a Chrome extension that integrates AI-powered assistance with Canvas LMS, helping students organize their academic workflow, track assignments, and manage their studies more effectively.

Key Features

  • AI-powered academic assistance
  • Automatic Canvas data extraction
  • Assignment tracking and prioritization
  • Grade monitoring and analysis
  • Smart study recommendations
  • Privacy-focused design
