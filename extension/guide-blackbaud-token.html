What is Blackbaud Integration?

Blackbaud (MySchoolApp) is the system your school uses for scheduling, attendance, and student information. Connecting it to Canvas AI Assistant allows you to:

📅

View Class Schedules

See your daily and weekly class schedules directly in the assistant.

🔔

Schedule Reminders

Get reminders about upcoming classes and schedule changes.

✨

Smarter AI Responses

The AI can reference your schedule when answering questions.

📊

Unified View

Combine Canvas assignments with your Blackbaud schedule.

ℹ️
Note: Blackbaud integration is optional. The Canvas AI Assistant works fully with just Canvas connected. Add Blackbaud for enhanced schedule features.
2

Connecting Your Blackbaud Account

1

Ensure Canvas is Connected

Before connecting Blackbaud, make sure you've successfully connected your Canvas account. The extension popup should show your Canvas data.

⚠️
Required: Canvas must be connected first. If you haven't done this, see the Canvas API Token Setup guide.
2

Open Extension Settings

Click the Canvas AI Assistant icon in your browser toolbar, then click the ⚙️ Settings button (gear icon) to open the settings page.

Canvas AI Assistant
📊 Status: Ready
⚙️ Settings ← Click here
3

Navigate to Authentication Page

In the settings sidebar, make sure you're on the "Authentication" section (it should be selected by default).

4

Find Blackbaud Connection Card

Scroll down the Authentication page until you see the "Blackbaud Connection" card. It should show "Not Connected" status initially.

Blackbaud Connection ❌ Not Connected

Sync your class schedules and student details

5

Click "Connect Blackbaud"

Click the "Connect Blackbaud" button. A new window will open for Blackbaud authentication.

6

Sign In to Blackbaud

In the popup window, sign in with your Blackbaud/MySchoolApp credentials. These are typically your school credentials.

ℹ️
Same as MySchoolApp: Use the same login you use for MySchoolApp or your school's Blackbaud portal.
7

Authorize Access

After logging in, Blackbaud will ask you to authorize Canvas AI Assistant to access your schedule data. Click "Allow" or "Authorize".

8

Connection Complete!

The popup will close and the settings page will update to show "✅ Connected". Your schedule data will begin syncing.

✅ Blackbaud connected successfully! Reload any Canvas tab to see your schedule data.
3

What You Can Do Now

With Blackbaud connected, try these features:

Ask the AI Assistant:

💬 "What classes do I have today?"
💬 "When is my next Math class?"
💬 "Show my schedule for this week"
💬 "What time does English start tomorrow?"
4

Disconnecting Blackbaud

If you need to disconnect your Blackbaud account:

1

Go to Settings → Authentication

2

Find the Blackbaud Connection card

3

Click "Disconnect" button

4

Confirm the disconnection when prompted

ℹ️
Note: Disconnecting Blackbaud won't affect your Canvas connection. Canvas AI Assistant will continue to work with Canvas data.

Having Trouble?

If you're experiencing problems connecting Blackbaud, make sure your school uses Blackbaud/MySchoolApp and that you have valid credentials. Check our Troubleshooting Guide for more help.
